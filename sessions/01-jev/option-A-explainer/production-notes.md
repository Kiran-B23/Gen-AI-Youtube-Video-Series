## Checks before recording

- **Terms:** Jev is always called a **model** or **decision model**, never "an AI that can't…". "LLM" is defined once (in "It decides") and used consistently after that.
- **Claims:** every claim is attributed to TypeSafe or a named tester, and the fine print is spoken. No "guaranteed" or "100%" of our own.
- **References:** "It matters later" points forward to the DRS/slider part, which exists.
- **Standalone:** no references to other videos. The next-video tease is only in the outro.
- **Facts to re-verify on the day:** launch date, $40M, HN points, platform list, signups paused, JevBench scores (63.3 / 30.3), the four-cents figure. Sources are in `../archive/v3/05-presenter-runbook.md` §7.
- **Table read** with the presenter before recording.

## Cut plan (if a take runs long)

1. Drop the resume-screener bullet.
2. Shorten the open-source challengers section to its first and last sentences.
3. Shorten the "tiny decisions" bullets to one example.

**Never cut** the fine print, the "format guarantee" line, or the umpire's-call rule. They pay off the hook.

- **Thumbnail idea:** the Jev wordmark and an OMR sheet with one shaded bubble, plus the text "NOT AN LLM?".
