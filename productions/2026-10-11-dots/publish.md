## Caption / description
A dot is OpenAI's always-on agent inside ChatGPT. You give it a goal, it keeps working between your chats on its own cloud computer
(with its own browser you can open and watch), and it brings results back for your review. You decide how much it can do alone with
four Custom rules levels, and some steps, like changing a password, always stay with you. Here's the catch: disconnecting an app
doesn't delete what the dot already learned from it; the only wipe is deleting the whole dot. The 3 AM bug example is an
illustration of the job OpenAI itself describes, not a recorded run; every feature claim is OpenAI's own (Help Center and
announcement linked below). Nothing here about plans, prices or availability: check OpenAI's page for your region.
