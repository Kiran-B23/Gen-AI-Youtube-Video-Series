# Research · OpenAI Dots (always-on agents in ChatGPT)

_Researched on 2026-10-11. Labels: company claim · independent · our test · community._
_The OpenAI Help Center page ("Getting started with your dot") returned 403 to our fetcher, so Help Center wording is taken from
outlets that quote it verbatim (DataCamp, eesel). Cross-checked where two outlets agree._

## One-line definition (by what it does)
A dot is an always-on AI agent inside ChatGPT: you give it a goal, connect the apps it needs and set what it may do alone,
and it keeps working on its own cloud computer after you close the chat, bringing results back for your review.

## Running-example candidates
- **A bug report in Slack overnight** (OpenAI's own example, S11): the dot investigates on its own computer, drafts a fix, and waits for approval. Chosen: it shows S1, S2, S4 in one story. Shown as an illustration, labelled "illustrative".
- A scientist's analysis rerun on new data (S11): less relatable to our audience.

## Analogy candidates
- A teammate on the night shift who leaves you a note and waits for your sign-off. Not used on screen as an analogy line; it shapes the visual (night → morning).

## The catch (limitations, risks, costs)
- Memory: disconnecting an app doesn't erase what it learned; only a full reset wipes it (S8).
- Access: Pro / Business Premium only; Pro not in the EEA, Switzerland or UK (S7).
- It can make mistakes; prompt-injection risk is reduced, not removed (S9).
- A permissions anecdote from a developer (eesel) is community, single-source: cut.

## Alternatives / competitors
- Codex and other agent harnesses already did this (S10). Meta's Muse is mentioned as a rival in coverage (trivia for a reel: cut).

## Viewer questions to answer
- What does it actually do that ChatGPT doesn't? (S1, S2)
- Can it do things without asking me? (S4, S5)
- What does it know about me, and can I delete it? (S8)
- Can I get it? (S7)

## Cut by the viewer-value filter (and why)
- DevDay venue, launch day, GPT-6 Astra model name: trivia, doesn't change what the viewer can do.
- "$100/month Pro" price: single secondary source (eesel), not confirmed by OpenAI pages we could read. Cut.
- The old script's "early tester's invoice" story: unsourced. Cut.
- Character / avatar picker, Meta Muse rivalry: no viewer value in a 60 s reel.
