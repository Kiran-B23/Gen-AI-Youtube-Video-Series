- Beats 11–14 (reworded 2026-10-27 at the user's request, option b, after the Goldfish could not tell "act without asking" from "act only when you asked") give each level its own short beat on a top-to-bottom ladder, and its own slot that shows the Help Center's exact label as it is spoken: "acts on its own, without asking you" = Take action without asking · "acts only on what your prompt already asked for" = Take action if pre-approved ("you explicitly requested the action in your prompt") · "asks you first, and waits for your okay" = Ask before taking action · "doesn't act … hands the job back to you" = Hand off to you (the step is completed by you). - Beat 16 names Slack as the app (2026-10-27, Goldfish: "Disconnect an app" had no referent; Slack is the app the hook connects, S14 "You can connect your dot to Slack").
- Nothing about plans, prices, regions, app counts or Teams (user rule).

## Read-aloud version (clean: exactly what the voice says, one paragraph per beat)

It's three A.M. A bug report lands in Slack.

By morning, a fix is waiting for you to review.

That's a dot: an always-on agent inside ChatGPT.

You give it a goal, and it keeps working between your chats.

How? Each dot gets its own cloud computer, with its own browser.

So it doesn't just talk about the bug. In OpenAI's own example, it writes the fix, and tests it.

You can open its computer any time and watch it work.

When it's done, the results come back to you, for review.

It can also run on a schedule, like checking your calendar every morning.

Now, the part that matters: you decide how much it can do alone.

There are four levels. At the top, it acts on its own, without asking you.

One step down, it acts only on what your prompt already asked for.

Next, it asks you first, and waits for your okay.

At the bottom, it doesn't act. It hands the job back to you.

And some things, like changing a password, it always leaves to you.

Now, here's the catch. Disconnect an app like Slack from your dot, and what it already read there stays.

To wipe that, you delete the whole dot. Chats, memories, schedules, all of it.

And it can still make mistakes, even while following your rules. So check its work.

My take? Start it on ask first, and loosen the rules as it earns your trust.
