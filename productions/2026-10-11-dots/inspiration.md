## Winners vs low performers
- Winners either show the dot **doing real work** (OpenAI, Futurepedia) or bring a **strong point of view** (PrimeTime, Fireship). Low-reach on-topic Shorts ([327 views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XY0mByYXJo4), [261 views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A-uUKLihlS8), [24 views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LdrNC44E6SM)) are generic "Dots explained" summaries with model names and rival names in the title, and no demo or catch in the framing.
- Winners name a consequence early (a fixed task, a price, a disaster); the low performers open on the product name.
- Nobody in the top results explains the **memory/reset catch** or the **four permission levels** in under a minute.

We deliberately sit below the winners' pace: the house rule is calm teaching, not a creator read._

## Gaps nobody covers
- What you actually control: the four Custom Rules levels, and what always stays with you.
- The memory catch: disconnecting an app doesn't erase what the dot learned; only deleting the dot does.
- A plain verdict on who should switch it on, and how (start on "ask first").

## Our angle (one sentence)
Show one overnight job the way OpenAI pitches it, then give the viewer the two things the launch videos skip: the control dial and the memory catch.
