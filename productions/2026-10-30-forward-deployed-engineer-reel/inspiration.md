## Winners vs low performers
- **Winners name a stake in the first second** (money: "$1M"; proof: "15 interviews"). The Salesforce Short opens on jargon over a static talking head, zero cuts, and stalls at 3K. Northslope survives on a real FDE but has no hook.
- **The concrete client problem is what the long-form winners promise** (the 4.8 h project starts from one client's legacy system and gets 220K views), but **no Short shows one**: they list "a bank, a hospital, a manufacturer" and move on.
- **Honest fit beats pure hype:** the top Short's strongest stretch is the "who should skip it" ending (travel, meetings). That's the catch the official postings back (25–50 % travel).
- Winners move fast (3.2–3.4 w/s, a cut every ~2 s); our house pace is 2.3 w/s, so we earn retention with motion on one object, not speed.

## Winning archetype
_Career framing (Trend/"should you") wins the one real Short; long-form winners are Tips (interviews) and Concept (podcast). None explains the job as a mechanism on one example._ For our viewer (students and early-career devs who've seen the title everywhere and can't say what the job *is*), **Concept** answers the real question; the career stake ("should I aim for it?") lives in the "why it matters to you" and takeaway beats.

## Pace and length targets
_Winners' median (shorts): 100–172 s · 3.2–3.4 w/s · 21–28 cuts/min → our target: ~75–90 s at the house 2.3 w/s (~170–200 words); a visual change every 2–3 s, achieved by the running object changing state, not by meme cuts._

## Gaps nobody covers
- **What the job does, shown on one real-looking problem** (from "the demo works" to "it works in their building").
- **Why AI companies suddenly need this role**: models don't plug into a company's old systems on their own.
- **The honest split** (most of it is integration and talking to people, not model work), and the real median pay instead of "$1M".
- **What to practise this month** if you want in (no Short gives an action).

## Our angle (one sentence)
Follow one AI demo that works on stage and breaks inside a real company, and watch a forward deployed engineer make it work — so you can say what the job is, why it exploded, and whether it's for you.
