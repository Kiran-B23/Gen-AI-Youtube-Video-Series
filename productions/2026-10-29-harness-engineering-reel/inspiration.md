## Gaps nobody covers
1. **The measured answer to "is it the model or the harness?"** HarnessTax (S8, Sept 2026): swapping popular harnesses barely moved success, but moved the bill up to 5×. In no watched video.
2. **The failure first, on mute**: the agent saying "Done" while a test is red (S4, S5).
3. **Guides before + checks after** (S3) shown on one task.
4. **The habit** (S1): every mistake becomes a rule or a check — the thing to do tomorrow.

## Our angle (one sentence)
One task the agent calls "Done" while its test is red: watch it fail fast, then replay it slow with the harness named piece by piece (a map before, a check after, the loop), and learn from the newest study that the harness you pick mostly changes price, not success, so the part worth engineering is the checks you write for your own project (owner-revised 2026-10-30).

## Effects plan to carry into art direction (devices, rebuilt in house style on our own object)
