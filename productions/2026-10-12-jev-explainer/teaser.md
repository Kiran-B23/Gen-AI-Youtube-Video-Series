## Animation plan (what the picture does)
Layout: characters stand in the left third (M) and right edge (L); the **stage** (the SMS and Jev's answers) fills the
centre 60%. Characters are SVG with a small set of parts (head, torso, arms, mouth shapes), lip-synced to the voice
from the Whisper timings (open/closed/round mouth on vowels, closed on silence).

| # | Section colour | Characters do | The stage does (motion verbs) | Beat words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | off-white | L walks in from the right, **holds up the phone**, looks at M | A big phone slides up centre; the SMS **TYPES OUT** in a bubble | "message" → bubble types |
| 2 | off-white → navy flash | M **glances** and points | A red **SCAM** stamp **STAMPS** on the bubble; "96%" **COUNTS UP**; a timer chip "0.1 s" pops | "Scam" → stamp · "Ninety-six" → count · "second" → timer |

Motion rules: build → breathe → resolve per beat; first motion 0.1–0.3 s after the scene starts; entrances longer than
exits; a character gesture lands on the same beat word as the stage element it points at.

## What this teaser proves (and what it doesn't)
- Proves: 16:9 layout in the house style, two characters with lip-sync and gestures, two-voice dialogue in one take,
  the step-by-step illustration language for the long-form demo.
- Doesn't prove: chapter structure, the 10–12 min pace, the calibration and cost scenes (those come with the full script).

## Engine work this needs (one-time, reused by every long-form video)
1. **16:9 in the studio**: captions, safe zones, stills and QA for 1920×1080 (today they assume 9:16).
2. **Character rig**: SVG characters as a reusable kit (`studio/templates/characters/`) with poses
   (stand, point, hold phone, count, warn, thumbs up, walk in) and mouth shapes driven by word timings.
3. **Two-speaker voice**: `voice.py` sends one Gemini multi-speaker request and splits the take by speaker.
