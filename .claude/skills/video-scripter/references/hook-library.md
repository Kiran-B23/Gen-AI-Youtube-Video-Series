# Hook library

A hook = **first spoken line + first frame**. It must be true, and the video must pay it off.
Score every hook 1–5 on: **clarity** (gets it at once?), **curiosity** (must keep watching?),
**accuracy** (fully true?), **first-frame** (works muted?). Ship hooks scoring ≥16/20.

| # | Formula | Example (tech) | First-frame idea | Suits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Paradox** | "This AI model can't write a single word, and it's blowing up." | A chatbot typing vs a single "✓ billing" chip | Concept, Launch |
| 2 | **Striking number** | "Two words just became seven tokens." | "2 → 7" in giant type | Concept, Risk, Story |
| 3 | **Curiosity gap** | "There's a setting in ChatGPT almost nobody turns on." | A blurred toggle with a "?" | Tips |
| 4 | **You've been doing it wrong** | "You're prompting AI like it's Google, and that's why it fails." | A search-bar prompt crossed out | Myth, Tips |
| 5 | **Time / effort contrast** | "This took me 3 hours. AI did it in 40 seconds." | A split screen with two timers | Launch, Tutorial, Story |
| 6 | **Story cold-open** | "It's 3 AM. You're asleep. Your AI just finished your work." | A dark bedroom, the phone lights up | Story, Risk |
| 7 | **Question the viewer has** | "Is Cursor actually worth $20 a month?" | The price tag with a big "?" | Launch, Comparison, Trend |
| 8 | **Contrarian** | "RAG is not dead. You're just doing it wrong." | "RAG ☠️" headline torn in half | Myth, Trend |
| 9 | **Before → after demo** | "Watch this blurry screenshot become working code." | The before image, a cursor on "go" | Launch, Tutorial |
| 10 | **Warning / catch first** | "Before you connect your email to an AI agent, watch this." | A ⚠️ over an inbox | Risk |
| 11 | **Free alternative** | "Everyone's paying for this. There's a free version that runs on your laptop." | A price tag vs a laptop | Launch |
| 12 | **Explain like I'm busy** | "Model Context Protocol, in 45 seconds." | A 0:45 countdown + a logo-free diagram | Concept, Tutorial |
| 13 | **Ranking / showdown** | "I tested 4 AI coding agents on the same bug. One wasn't close." | 4 cards, one glowing | Comparison |
| 14 | **Myth-buster** | "No, AI doesn't 'read' your text. It reads numbers." | Text dissolving into digits | Myth, Concept |
| 15 | **Future stakes** | "In a year, your coworker might be an AI that never logs off." | An empty office, one screen still on | Trend |
| 16 | **Relatable pain** | "Every freelancer has forgotten to send an invoice. Now AI does it for you." | A sticky note "Invoice??" falling | Tutorial, Story |
| 17 | **Insider secret** | "This is the prompt structure AI labs use internally." | A redacted doc revealing one line | Tips |
| 18 | **One-line definition** | "An agent is just an LLM in a loop, with tools." | The loop drawn in one stroke | Concept |

Each archetype file lists its best formulas. Write hooks from those first, then make each one beat the winners' hooks from `inspiration.md`.

Avoid: "Hey guys", "In this video", "Did you know…" (weak), clickbait the video doesn't pay off,
and hooks that depend on a logo or someone's face.
