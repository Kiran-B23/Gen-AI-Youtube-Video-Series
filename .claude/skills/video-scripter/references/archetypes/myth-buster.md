# Myth-buster: "Is what I believe true?"

**Signals:** a widespread belief about AI; "actually", "doesn't", "stop", "myth", "everyone thinks",
"you've been doing it wrong".
**Answered when:** the viewer has *seen* proof that the belief is wrong (or only half right), and knows
what's true and what to do instead.

**Best hooks:**
- #14 myth-buster: state the belief, then contradict it
- #4 "you've been doing it wrong"
- #8 contrarian

The myth itself is the hook: the first line states the belief being challenged.

**Running example:** **one test** that exposes the myth live, small enough to show in ~5 s. For example,
asking a model to count the letters in a word; showing the same prompt twice giving two answers.

## Reel beat sheet (30–50 s)
| Beat | Time | Job | Must contain | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The myth | 0–2 s | State the belief people hold | The belief in its common wording, marked as the myth | `[TITLE]` |
| Why it feels true | 2–8 s | Respect the viewer | The everyday experience behind it | `[SCREEN]` / `[TITLE]` |
| The proof | 8–20 s | Break it, on screen | The test, run live; a visible result | `[SCREEN]` / `[TERMINAL]` / `[ANIMATE]` |
| The real picture | 20–32 s | What's actually going on | The mechanism in one sentence + one visual | `[ANIMATE]` |
| Do this instead | 32–42 s | The behaviour change | 1–2 concrete actions | `[LIST]` / `[VERDICT]` |
| CTA | last 3–5 s | Invite the next myth | "What AI myth should I test next?" | `[CTA]` |

## Explainer outline (8–15 min)
Cold open on the myth + the live failure (≤ 30 s) → why people believe it (10%) → 2–3 tests, each harder
(40%; 🔁 at ~25%) → what's really happening (25%) → when the myth is partly true (10%; honesty) →
what to do + CTA (10%).

**Tone (→ voice style):** "Playful and confident, a little mischievous on the myth, then clear and reassuring on the truth."

**Must-haves:**
- The myth is stated fairly (not a straw man).
- The proof is shown, not just asserted.
- Say where the myth is partly right.
- The fix is an action.

**Don'ts:**
- No mocking the viewer.
- No "everyone is wrong" without evidence.
- No proof that depends on a cherry-picked run (re-run it and say so).

## Worked mini example (reel)
| Beat | Line | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Myth | "AI reads your sentence word by word. It doesn't." | `[TITLE]` |
| Feels true | "It answers like it read every word, so it must have, right?" | `[SCREEN]` |
| Proof | "Ask it how many r's are in 'strawberry'. Models often get it wrong." | `[SCREEN]` |
| Real picture | "It sees chunks called tokens, not letters, so it can't simply count them." | `[ANIMATE]` |
| Do instead | "For letters and counting, ask it to spell the word out first, or use code." | `[LIST]` |
| CTA | "Which AI myth should I test next?" | `[CTA]` |
