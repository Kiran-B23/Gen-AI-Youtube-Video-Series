# Risk / warning: "Am I exposed?"

**Signals:** prompt injection, data leaks, privacy, deepfake scams, jailbreaks, hallucinated facts,
agents with access to email/files; "danger", "risk", "attack", "careful", "before you…".
**Answered when:** the viewer understands how the risk happens (simply), knows whether it applies to
them, and has 2–3 concrete protections.

**Best hooks:**
- #10 warning first: "Before you connect your email to an AI agent, watch this."
- #6 story cold-open
- #2 striking number (only when sourced)

**Running example:** **one realistic scenario**, shown from the attacker's input to the harm. Use a
sourced real incident, or a safe demo in our own sandbox labelled as such. Never a working exploit
recipe.

## Reel beat sheet (35–55 s)
| Beat | Time | Job | Must contain | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warning hook | 0–2 s | The stake, personally | What could happen to *you* | `[TITLE]` |
| How it happens | 2–15 s | The mechanism, simply | The scenario step by step at a high level (no payloads) | `[ANIMATE]` / `[SCREEN]` |
| It's real | 15–24 s | Evidence | A sourced incident or a safe sandbox demo | `[NUMBER]` / `[SCREEN]` |
| Who's at risk | 24–30 s | Help them self-assess | "If you use ___ with ___, this applies" | `[LIST]` |
| Protect yourself | 30–44 s | 2–3 concrete actions | Settings, habits or tools; specific | `[LIST]` |
| CTA | last 3–5 s | Share or comment | "Send this to someone who uses ___" or a question | `[CTA]` |

## Explainer outline (8–15 min)
Cold open on the scenario (≤ 30 s) → how it works (25%) → real cases, sourced (20%; 🔁 at ~25%) →
who's affected and how much (15%) → protections, from easiest to strongest (25%) → what the
companies are doing, plus a CTA (10%).

**Tone (→ voice style):** "Calm, serious and caring, like a friend warning you: no panic, slower on the protections."

**Must-haves:**
- Every incident and number is sourced (`research.md`).
- Severity is stated honestly.
- Protections are concrete.
- Demos are run only in our own sandbox.

**Don'ts:**
- No fear-mongering.
- No step-by-step attack instructions or payloads.
- No real victims' names or faces.
- No naming a company as "hacked" without a source.

## Worked mini example (reel, generic)
| Beat | Line | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | "An AI agent that reads your email can be given orders by a stranger." | `[TITLE]` |
| How | "Someone sends you an email with hidden instructions. Your agent reads it… and follows them." | `[ANIMATE]` |
| Real | "It's called prompt injection, and security researchers rank it a top risk for AI apps. [S1]" | `[NUMBER]` |
| Who | "If your agent can read email and also send or click, this applies to you." | `[LIST]` |
| Protect | "Keep agents read-only, require approval before actions, and don't give them all your accounts." | `[LIST]` |
| CTA | "Send this to the friend who just hooked AI up to their inbox." | `[CTA]` |
