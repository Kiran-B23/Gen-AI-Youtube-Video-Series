# Use-case / story: "What can this do for me?"

**Signals:** "I built", "I automated", "I replaced", "how X uses AI", a case study, a result with numbers.
**Answered when:** the viewer sees a believable before → after for one real person or team, understands
roughly how it was done, and pictures their own version.

**Best hooks:**
- #6 story cold-open
- #5 time contrast: "This took 3 hours. Now it takes 4 minutes."
- #16 relatable pain

**Running example:** **the one person and the one task**, with real numbers (our test, or sourced),
from the pain to the result.

## Reel beat sheet (40–60 s)
| Beat | Time | Job | Must contain | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cold open on the result | 0–3 s | The "after" | The outcome with a number | `[NUMBER]` / `[SCREEN]` |
| The before | 3–12 s | The pain the viewer recognises | A specific, relatable struggle | `[TITLE]` / `[SCREEN]` |
| What was built | 12–26 s | The solution at a glance | 2–3 parts and how they connect, with no deep how-to | `[ANIMATE]` / `[SCREEN]` |
| The numbers | 26–34 s | Proof | Time/cost/errors before vs after, sourced or "our test" | `[VS]` / `[NUMBER]` |
| The catch | 34–40 s | Honesty | What still needs a human, what broke | `[VERDICT]` |
| Your version + CTA | last 5–8 s | Transfer it to the viewer | "You could do this for ___", then a question | `[CTA]` |

## Explainer outline (8–15 min)
Cold open on the result (≤ 30 s) → the before, in detail (15%) → the build, a walkthrough of the real
system (40%; 🔁 at ~25% "and then it broke") → the results and costs (15%) → the lessons and catch (15%)
→ how to start your own + CTA (10%).

**Tone (→ voice style):** "Warm storyteller who's still a bit amazed: relaxed on the before, building energy towards the result."

**Must-haves:**
- A real case (ours or sourced).
- Before/after numbers.
- The catch.
- A "your version" transfer line.

**Don'ts:**
- No invented testimonials or numbers.
- No hiding failures.
- No turning it into a full tutorial in a reel.

## Worked mini example (reel, illustrative: run it for real before publishing)

|---|---|---|
