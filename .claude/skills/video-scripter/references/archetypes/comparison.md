# Comparison / showdown: "Which one should I use?"

**Signals:** "X vs Y", "best", "which", "compared", "tested", 2+ named tools or models.
**Answered when:** the viewer knows which option fits *their* case, based on evidence they saw, not just
an opinion.

**Best hooks:**
- #13 ranking/showdown: "I gave 3 AI tools the same bug. One wasn't close."
- #7 the viewer's question
- #5 time contrast
- the surprise result first: "The free one won."

**Running example:** **one fair test**, with the same input, rules and scoring for every option. Say the
test out loud. Results are labelled "our test" with the date (in `research.md`).

## Reel beat sheet (40–60 s)
| Beat | Time | Job | Must contain | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–2 s | The question or the surprise | The contenders + a stake | `[VS]` / `[TITLE]` |
| The test | 2–8 s | Make it fair and specific | The same input for all; what "winning" means | `[TITLE]` / `[SCREEN]` |
| Round 1 | 8–20 s | First criterion (usually quality) | The outputs side by side | `[VS]` / `[SCREEN]` |
| Round 2 | 20–30 s | Second criterion (speed or cost) | A number per option | `[NUMBER]` / `[VS]` |
| The surprise | 30–38 s | What the viewer didn't expect | A twist: a weakness of the favourite, a strength of the underdog | `[VERDICT]` |
| Verdict by use case | 38–48 s | Pick per person, not one winner | "Use A if…, B if…" | `[LIST]` |
| CTA | last 3–5 s | A poll | "Which would you pick: A or B?" | `[CTA]` |

## Explainer outline (8–15 min)
Cold open on the surprise (≤ 30 s) → the contenders, in one line each (5%) → the test rules (5%) →
3–5 rounds (60%; a scoreboard after each; 🔁 loop at ~25% "round 3 flips everything") →
cost/limits/privacy (15%) → the verdict matrix + CTA (10%).

**Tone (→ voice style):** "Energetic referee: punchy on each round result, fair and measured on the verdict."

**Must-haves:**
- The same test for every option, stated on screen.
- The results come from a real run ("our test", dated) or a sourced benchmark.
- The verdict is split by use case.
- Prices and versions are current on the recording date.

**Don'ts:**
- No winner without evidence.
- No cherry-picking one lucky output.
- No logos (use plain-text names).
- No outdated pricing.
