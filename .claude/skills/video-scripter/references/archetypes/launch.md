# Tool / model launch: "What is it, and should I care?"

**Signals:** a newly released model, feature, app or API; "new", "just dropped", "launched", "announced".
**Answered when:** the viewer knows what it *does* (in one sentence), has seen it work once, knows the
catch, and can decide "try it / skip it".

**Best hooks:**
- #9 before → after demo
- #1 paradox (when the tool does something unusual)
- #5 time contrast
- #7 the viewer's own question: "Is X worth switching to?"
- #11 free alternative

Lead with **what it does, shown**, never with who made it or when.

**Running example:** one real task the target user has, run through the tool live (or shown from the
company's own demo, labelled as such).

## Reel beat sheet (35–55 s)
| Beat | Time | Job | Must contain | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–2 s | The result first | The most surprising output, or what it does in ≤ 8 words | `[SCREEN]` / `[TITLE]` |
| What it is | 2–8 s | Define it by what it does | "It's a ___ that ___." No dates, venues or HQs | `[TITLE]` |
| Demo | 8–22 s | The running example, start to finish | The input → the output; the real UI or a labelled illustration | `[SCREEN]` / `[TERMINAL]` |
| Why it's different | 22–30 s | The one thing it does that the alternatives don't | One sourced number or a visible difference | `[VS]` / `[NUMBER]` |
| The catch | 30–38 s | The honest limit | Price, access, accuracy, privacy, "company claim" label | `[VERDICT]` |
| Verdict | 38–45 s | Who should try it, and who shouldn't | A person-level recommendation | `[VERDICT]` / `[LIST]` |
| CTA | last 3–5 s | Comment prompt | "Would you use it for ___?" | `[CTA]` |

## Explainer outline (8–15 min)
| Section | Share | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | ≤ 30 s | The best output, plus "but there's a catch, stay for it" |
| What it is | 10% | By what it does; who it's for |
| Hands-on | 35% | 2–3 real tasks of rising difficulty, on camera. 🔁 Open loop at ~25% |
| How it works (briefly) | 10% | Only what explains the behaviour |
| Vs the alternatives | 15% | The same task on the closest alternative |
| The catch | 15% | Limits, cost, privacy, what we couldn't verify. 🔁 Pay-off at ~60% |
| Verdict + CTA | 10% | Who should switch, who shouldn't |

**Tone (→ voice style):** "Genuinely excited but honest, like a creator who just tried it: fast on the demo, calm and serious on the catch."

**Must-haves:**
- The demo is shown, not just described.
- Company claims are worded as claims ("they say…").
- The catch is included.
- It is introduced by what it does.

**Don'ts:**
- No launch trivia (date, event, funding, HQ, version history).
- No "game-changer".
- No verdict without a demo.

## Worked mini example (reel, generic)
| Beat | Line | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | "This model never writes a word. It only decides." | `[TITLE]` |
| What it is | "It's a model that picks one answer from a list you give it." | `[TITLE]` |
| Demo | "Give it a support ticket… it returns one label: billing." | `[SCREEN]` |
| Different | "One label instead of a paragraph means fewer tokens, so it's faster and cheaper. [S2]" | `[VS]` |
| Catch | "But it can't explain itself, and the speed numbers are the company's own." | `[VERDICT]` |
| Verdict | "Great for routing and tagging; useless for writing." | `[LIST]` |
| CTA | "What would you make it decide?" | `[CTA]` |
