# Tips / hacks list: "What can I do better today?"

**Signals:** "tips", "tricks", "hacks", "mistakes", "settings", "prompts", a number ("3 ways…").
**Answered when:** the viewer can apply each tip right after watching, and has seen each one make a
difference.

**Best hooks:**
- open on the **payoff of the best tip** (the before → after)
- #3 curiosity gap: "the setting almost nobody turns on"
- #4 "you've been doing it wrong"

Don't open with "here are 3 tips": show the result of one.

**Running example:** one task the viewer does often (writing an email, summarising a doc, debugging).
Every tip improves **that same task**, so the before/after is comparable.

## Reel beat sheet (35–55 s; 3 tips; 5 max for 60 s+)
| Beat | Time | Job | Must contain | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–2 s | The best tip's payoff | A visible before → after | `[VS]` / `[SCREEN]` |
| Tip 1 | 2–14 s | Problem → tip → result | The problem (1 line), the exact tip, a mini before/after | `[SCREEN]` / `[CODE]` / `[VS]` |
| Tip 2 | 14–26 s | Same shape | Same | same |
| Tip 3 (the best) | 26–40 s | Save the strongest for last | The hook's payoff, fully shown | same |
| Recap | 40–45 s | The screenshot moment | All tips as one list | `[LIST]` |
| CTA | last 3–5 s | Save or comment | "Save this" or "Which one are you trying first?" | `[CTA]` |

Mid-list turn: at tip 2, signal that the best is coming ("but the last one changed everything").

## Explainer outline (8–15 min)
Cold open on the best result (≤ 30 s) → 5–8 tips grouped into 2–3 themes (75%; each with a demo; a turn
at ~50%) → the mistake to avoid (10%) → the recap card + CTA (10%).

**Tone (→ voice style):** "Upbeat and practical, like sharing secrets with a friend: quick on the problem, excited on each result."

**Must-haves:**
- Every tip is shown working on the same running task.
- Each tip is one specific action, not general advice.
- There is a recap list.

**Don'ts:**
- No filler tips ("be specific").
- No more than 5 tips in a reel.
- No tips you haven't tried.

## Worked mini example (reel, generic)
| Beat | Line | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | "Same prompt, two answers. The only difference is one line." | `[VS]` |
| Tip 1 | "Vague answers? Tell it who the reader is: 'explain to a new hire'." | `[SCREEN]` |

| Tip 3 | "Best one: paste an example of the answer you want. It copies the style." | `[VS]` |
| Recap | "Reader, format, example." | `[LIST]` |
| CTA | "Save this. Which one will you try first?" | `[CTA]` |
