# Trend / opinion: "Where is this going?"

**Signals:** "future of", "replacing", "is X dead", "bubble", "will AI…", an industry shift, a debate.
**Answered when:** the viewer understands the shift, has seen the evidence on both sides, knows our
stance and why, and knows what to do *now*.

**Best hooks:**
- #8 contrarian: "RAG isn't dead."
- #15 future stakes
- #7 the viewer's question

Make a claim the video defends; never a vague "what if".

**Running example:** **one concrete signal** that embodies the trend (a product change, a job task, a
price drop), returned to at the end.

## Reel beat sheet (35–55 s)
| Beat | Time | Job | Must contain | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–2 s | A clear stance or stake | The claim in ≤ 10 words | `[TITLE]` |
| The signal | 2–10 s | Make it concrete | One real, sourced example of the shift | `[SCREEN]` / `[NUMBER]` |
| Evidence ×2 | 10–26 s | Support the stance | Two more sourced points, one visual each | `[NUMBER]` / `[VS]` |
| The other side | 26–34 s | Steel-man the opposing view | The best counter-argument, fairly | `[VS]` |
| Our stance | 34–42 s | The resolved view | One sentence + why | `[VERDICT]` |
| What to do now | 42–48 s | Make it actionable | One skill, habit or decision | `[TITLE]` / `[LIST]` |
| CTA | last 3–5 s | Debate | "Agree or disagree?" | `[CTA]` |

## Explainer outline (8–15 min)
Cold open on the stance (≤ 30 s) → what's changing (15%) → the evidence, 3–4 points (35%; 🔁 at ~25%) →
the strongest counter-arguments (20%) → our stance and what would change our mind (10%) → what to do,
by audience (10%) → CTA.

**Tone (→ voice style):** "Thoughtful and confident, a little provocative at the start, fair and grounded on the other side."

**Must-haves:**
- Every evidence point is sourced and dated.
- The other side is presented fairly.
- Predictions are labelled as opinion.
- There is an actionable takeaway.

**Don'ts:**
- No doom or hype without evidence.
- No "X is dead" clickbait the video doesn't defend.
- No quoting people out of context.

## Worked mini example (reel, generic; source each point before use)
| Beat | Line | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | "Prompt engineering isn't dying. It's becoming spec writing." | `[TITLE]` |
| Signal | "Agent tools now ask for a plan file before they write code. [S1]" | `[SCREEN]` |
| Evidence | "And models follow long, structured instructions far better than they used to. [S2]" | `[NUMBER]` |

| Stance | "Tricks are fading. Clear thinking on paper isn't." | `[VERDICT]` |
| Do now | "Practise writing a one-page spec before you prompt." | `[TITLE]` |
| CTA | "Agree or disagree?" | `[CTA]` |
