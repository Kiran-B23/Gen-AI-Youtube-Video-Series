# Dots: the AI that works while you sleep (story-driven · Day-to-Night theme)

One line per scene; visuals trigger on the spoken words.
Voice: Gemini single take (temporary). Record your own s01…s11 into `voice/` to replace it.
Sources: OpenAI announcement (Sept 29 2026), OpenAI Help Center (availability, privacy & safety FAQ), news reports (Meta's Muse).

### s01: Hook

It's three A.M. You're fast asleep. But your phone just lit up. A bug? Found, and a fix drafted. An invoice? Ready for approval. Your morning plan? Done. Meet Dots, OpenAI's new always-on AI agents.

*Delivery: hushed and curious at first, then excited on "Meet Dots"*

### s02: Core idea

Here's the big idea. ChatGPT waits for you to type. A dot doesn't. It gets its own computer, and keeps working, day and night, even after you close the chat.

*Delivery: punchy contrast on "A dot doesn't"*

### s03: Analogy

Think of it like this. ChatGPT is a smart friend you call. A dot is an assistant at the next desk, who stays back after everyone's gone home. Oh, and there's a privacy catch. Stay till the end for that one.

*Delivery: storytelling, a little teasing on "privacy catch"*

### s04: Setup

Setting one up is simple. Name your dot, and pick a character. Which one would you pick? Tell me below! Then connect it to over four thousand apps. Give it a goal, it works on its own, and brings the result back for your approval.

*Delivery: friendly walkthrough, playful poll question, wow on "four thousand"*

### s05: Story

One early tester forgot to send an invoice. His dot noticed, prepared the invoice, and sent it. Every freelancer just felt that.

*Delivery: relatable, laugh on the last line*

### s06: Personas

So, what would your dot do? A developer's dot reads feedback, builds and tests the fix, and hands over a finished pull request. A creator's dot finds the best clip moments and drafts the post. And for everyone else? A morning review of your calendar.

*Delivery: brisk, playful*

### s07: Channels

And you can talk to it anywhere. In ChatGPT, in Slack, in Teams, or just call it. The more you work together, the more it learns how you think.

*Delivery: easy, warm*

### s08: Safety

But is it safe? You set the rules. Let it act without asking, act only if pre-approved, ask first, or hand things off to you. Pro tip: start with ask first, then loosen up as it earns your trust. And changing a password? That's always you.

*Delivery: calmer, reassuring; friendly on the pro tip*

### s09: The catch

Now, the catch. It's only on Pro and Business Premium, with one dot included. It can't call you yet. And the privacy part. You'd think disconnecting an app makes it forget. Nope! What it already learned stays. Only a full reset wipes it, and that deletes everything. Oh, and it can still make mistakes.

*Delivery: honest; playful "Nope!", slower on "What it already learned stays"*

### s10: Big picture

Zoom out. In twenty twenty-two, AI answered questions. Last year, it started doing tasks. Now, it works while you're offline. With rivals like Meta's Muse, the race has moved from chatbots, to AI coworkers.

*Delivery: building up to "AI coworkers"*

### s11: CTA

Save this for later. And tell me, what's the first job you'd give your dot? Follow for more AI breakdowns!

*Delivery: warm, inviting*
